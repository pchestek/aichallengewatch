Summary

The Babylon Bee sued members of the New Mexico State Ethics Commission to block enforcement of HB 182, which amended the state's Campaign Reporting Act to require a state-mandated disclaimer on "materially deceptive media" involving AI-generated or AI-manipulated content about political candidates or ballot measures. New Mexico's disclaimer requirement applies year-round rather than only near elections, and, according to the plaintiff sweeps in satire, parody, memes, flyers, and social media posts. Violations can carry fines of up to $20,000.
