Summary

X.AI LLC appealed the district court's denial of its motion for a preliminary injunction against Minnesota HF 1606 to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit. The district court had held that xAI failed to demonstrate irreparable harm, largely because of the length of time between the law's enactment and xAI's motion and the lack of irreparable harm.
