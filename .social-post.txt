babylon-bee-v-lopez
⚖️ Catch-up post, adding The Babylon Bee v. Lopez to the website. The challenged law was over use of AI for deceptive election materials; in January 2026 the District Court for the District of Hawai'i held the law unconstitutional under the First and Fourteenth Amendments. Decision at https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.hid.174048/gov.uscourts.hid.174048.47.0.pdf. The state did not appeal.
